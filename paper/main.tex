\section{Inverse problem}\label{sec:problem}
We consider the viscous Burgers equation
\begin{equation}
  u_t + u\,u_x = \nu\,u_{xx},
\end{equation}
with the viscosity $\nu$ unknown. The network is trained on the physics residual at $\Ncol$
